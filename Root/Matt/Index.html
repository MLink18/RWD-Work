Matt's Background

My name is Matthew, but I prefer Matt. I come from a hardworking christian family.
We have a very large vegetable farm in Salisbury, where I was raised.
I never was a fan of farmwork, but I'm not afraid of manual labor, more on that in a minute.
My love of technology comes from the family desktop computer, always wondered what the inside looked like,
and how it did what it did.

Back then we had Dial-up, had it until 2011, we ordered XplorNet "high speed"
Which really wasnt much better at all. Wasn't until I moved out in 2022 after graduation that I got my own wifi package
that could finally handle the games I played.

Over the years I would slowly attain for myself, more and more technology.
An iPod in elementary school, my first taste of portable music, but I had to
pay for individual songs, and as you can imagine, a 10 year old doesnt have much money.

Got my first phone at 12yo, worked in the garden for a few weeks to save enough to buy an iPhone 4 second hand
I kept working, even though I hated the garden work, eventually that got me my very own Playstation 3
It didnt work very good at all, discs wouldnt read, would crash intermittently, etc.

This right here, is what sparked my curiosity. I wasnt confident enough at the age i was,
to open it up and check things out myself. I dealt with that horrible PS3 for probably 3 years
until I decided I was switching to Xbox 360. All of my friends were on Xbox.

I worked more and more, got my Xbox 360 E model.
Worked awesome, not a single problem for the entire time I owned it
But I got it late, the Xbox One was out already, and for a few years now.

Sometime during that period between the PS3 and XBOX360, I got a really slow old laptop.
Dual core, 4gb ram, slowest machine ive ever used. I got ballsy one day and I took that thing apart.
And broke it.

But I learned something, I learned never to use force on delicate parts such as the motherboard and RAM.
At the tail end of 2017, I had found myself an XBOX One, and man was i ever proud of it.
As luck would have it, I managed to buy another broken gaming console, though it was even worse than my PS3
Not to get into details too deep, it would shut down and spit out discs by itself.

So I opened that too, very gently. This time i had actually found THE problem! There was corrosion
on some of the front panels solder pins. Now, I didnt know how to solder, but i was super proud to have
diagnosed the problem all on my own. (I still can't solder but its something id be willing to learn at this point)

Fast forward to October 2023, (not much more, I promise hehehe) I have built
my very own Gaming Desktop PC. Nothing crazy, but played everything I wanted.
This is where my passion for PC building and discussion skyrocketed to a place I never imagined I'd be.
The whole reason I am doing this course, stems from that crappy old ASUS Athlon Dual Core laptop,
Over the years I've been more focused on hardware specs and what different tiers of hardware can achieve.

Hopefully now, I have another "crappy laptop" moment with NBCC with the software side of things.
Im here to spark my imagination alight, create, break, fix, and most importantly, to learn.

TL;DR
I like computers.
