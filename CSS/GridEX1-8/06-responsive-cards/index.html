Exercise 6 · auto-fit and minmax()

Responsive product cards

Build a card grid that changes its number of columns to fit the screen, without a single media query.

Your tasks (in style.css)

  1. Make .cards a grid whose columns are at least 240px and share any extra space, with as many columns as fit. Use repeat(auto-fit, minmax(…)) and a 24px gap.
  2. Resize the browser from wide to phone size. The columns should drop from 4 to 3 to 2 to 1 by themselves.
  3. The “Add to cart” buttons don’t line up because the descriptions are different lengths. Make each .card a grid with rows auto 1fr auto (image, text, button) so every button sits at the bottom and a 12px gap.

Bonus: The second section only has two cards. Use auto-fit on .few.fit and auto-fill on .few.fill. On a wide screen, what’s the difference?

Hint

Cards in the same row are stretched to the same height by default. Once a card is a grid, a 1fr middle row soaks up the extra height and pushes the button down.
