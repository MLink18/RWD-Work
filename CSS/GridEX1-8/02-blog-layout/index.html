Exercise 2 · fr and repeat()

Blog layout

Build a page with a fixed sidebar and a flexible content area, then lay out the posts inside it.

Your tasks (in style.css)

  1. Make .layout a grid with a 260px sidebar and a main column that takes all the remaining space. Use a 32px gap.
  2. Make .posts a grid of three equal columns using repeat() and the fr unit, with a 24px gap.
  3. Resize your browser: the sidebar should stay 260px while the main column stretches and shrinks.

Bonus: Replace 260px with 1fr and the main column with 3fr. What changes when you resize now? Then try repeat(3, minmax(180px, 1fr)) on the posts and shrink the window.

Hint

1fr means “one share of the leftover space.” A fixed track plus one 1fr track gives the classic sidebar layout.
