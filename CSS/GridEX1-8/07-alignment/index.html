Exercise 7 · Aligning items

Centring and alignment

Practise the difference between moving items inside their cells (*-items, *-self) and moving the whole grid (*-content).

Your tasks (in style.css)

  1. Part A: Make .stage a grid (it already has a height) and centre .login both ways with one property.
  2. Part B: Make .icons a grid with 4 columns of 120px, rows of 120px and a 12px gap.
  3. Centre the whole icon grid horizontally inside .icons (move the grid, not the items).
  4. The icons are 60px, smaller than their 120px cells. Centre every icon inside its cell.
  5. Move just the .special icon to the top-right corner of its cell.

Bonus: Turn on the DevTools grid overlay for .icons so you can see the cells. Try justify-content: space-between and space-evenly.

Hint

justify-* works along the row (left–right). align-* works along the column (up–down). place-items sets both at once.
